01 — THE KEY THAT MAKES EVERYTHING CLICK

The Master Law

The face owns valence and the discrete label. Every other channel owns arousal plus exactly one modulator. The richest constructs marry the face's valence to a non-face arousal or direction axis the face physically cannot give — that un-measurable-alone property is the “greater than the sum.”

Windowed · shared space Immersive-only · aura open Gated on keystone K6

Arousal is easy; valence is hard — repeated across vision (CCC), voice (Juslin & Laukka) and body (Wallbott). So the face carries the weak-arousal, strong-valence load, and every other channel corroborates arousal or adds a direction the face can't reach.

F1

Composure-under-load

Channel-Divergence of arousal proxies
A−
F × E→ +H / VWindowed MVV

Anchor: Gross & Levenson 1993 — suppression → flat face + blink↑ + sympathetic arousal (SCL Δ 0.86 vs 0.33 µS, F(1,81)=6.19, p<.05). The app has no autonomic channel, so it proxies arousal behaviorally.

“Your Persona face reads calm, but blink-rate and hand energy are elevated — composed but activated; possible regulation. Confidence: moderate.” NEVER “concealing.” This is a proxy-of-a-proxy, not validated suppression.
Computation sketch
Detect near-neutral facial valence while arousal proxies diverge upward — blink-rate delta↑ (+ hand-energy / self-touch↑ and/or vocal F0/intensity↑). A windowed cross-channel divergence on the shared arousal axis; a hysteretic named state, not a per-frame flip. The 15 Hz boundary keeps it from becoming micro-expression lie-detection.

B+

F3

Fatigue ⇆ Engagement

ONE coupled meter, flips on the blink sign
est.

Anchor: Wierwille 1994 / Dinges PERCLOS (3 SD over baseline); Maffei 2018.

The congruence engine — the literal “greater than the sum”

tightens + brightens on agreement
widens + desaturates on conflict

Channel agreement becomes a computed scalar: consensus tightens confidence and raises intensity; divergence becomes a named state, never averaged away.

Arousal consensus — signs agree → confidence rises (reliability-weighted sign consensus, ship first)
“Composed but activated” — calm face, restless body: a divergence event, quarantined into a named state
“Signals conflict — low confidence” — never average a face-positive / body-negative split (Aviezer: the body wins at peak intensity)

Dynamic weighting: as facial intensity rises, decrease the face's reliability weight and raise motion/head/blink — a principled, one-file replacement for the fixed 0.35 FER+ weight.

05 — THE WORKED EXAMPLE · THE REUSABLE HONESTY WIDGET

What a frown plus a lowered head can tell us

The motivating question, resolved as a narrowing ladder. Each step lights up, the ruled-out confound greys with a strikethrough, and the app narrates the claim getting narrower — showing the machine thinking, never asserting. This same widget drives cognitive-load, fatigue, and every fusion mode.

0
Step 0 · pitch-correct — the imposter

Head-down geometry fakes a lowered brow

Looking down foreshortens the face into a V-brow — a geometric AU4 that isn't a real frown (Witkower & Tracy 2019/2020, the “AU4 imposter”). Strip it first.

“Brow looks lowered, but your head is down — correcting for that.” NEVER “angry” at this step.
1
Step 1 · brow morphology

What kind of lowered brow?

AU1+15 oblique → sadness lean AU4+5/7 → anger lean AU4 alone → effort lean
“Lowered brow: effort, displeasure or dejection — ambiguous; valence mildly negative, arousal uncertain.”
2
Step 2 · motivational direction — the new primitive

Approach or withdrawal? The split valence can't make

Anger is negative-valence but approach-motivated; sadness/shame are withdrawal (Harmon-Jones & Allen 1998). Motor energy + head orientation is the discriminator.

high motor + expansive → anger-approach low motor + collapse → dejection-withdrawal
“Leans [dejection-withdrawal / anger-approach].”
3
Step 3 · head-orientation proxy 🔒 gaze walled off

Concentration or shame? — may stay unresolved

The clean split needs gaze — and the app cannot read gaze or its target (system-side). So it uses a coarse head-orientation proxy and hedges honestly.

head toward content → focused effort head down-and-away → withdrawal gaze direction → concentration vs shame
“Head oriented toward your content → leans focused effort; turned down-and-away → leans withdrawal — low confidence, we can't see your gaze, may stay unresolved.”
✓
Steps 4–5 · blink / load + time-on-task

Final hedge, with the confound still on screen

Suppressed blinks + hand-tension → effort; elevated + agitated → negative arousal; closures + droop → fatigue/dejection. Always show the confound being ruled out + an epistemic-confidence meter separate from intensity.

“Most consistent with focused effort”  — or —  “leans low-arousal dejection.”
06 — THE HONESTY BAR AS A PRODUCT FEATURE

What we will never ship

The refusals are not fine print — they are the differentiator. The app publishes what it will not infer, each with a citation or a hardware wall. It is the pre-emptive answer to “why not detect X?” and, post the Nov-2025 App-Review third-party-AI-consent revision, on-device-only is a concrete moat.

✗
Genuine-vs-fake smile (Duchenne / AU6)
An intensity artifact, not a genuineness marker (Girard 2021) — use F6 Corroborated-Positivity instead.
✗
Gaze-direction “tells” → deception
Wiseman 2012 debunk; and gaze is walled off anyway.
✗
Micro-expression “leakage”
~2% of expressions, don't separate truth/lies (Porter & ten Brinke 2008); 15 Hz can't sample them — the boundary that keeps F1 from becoming lie-detection.
✗
Head-tilt (roll) dictionary
Sign-unstable, smile/gender-confounded (Otta 1994; Torrance 2020).
✗
Nod / shake → propositional yes/no
Culture-coded; the waggle inverts in India / Bulgaria / Greece (McClave 2000).
✗
Pupil × anything
No API + we refuse to fake it — and we say why, to pre-empt “why not pupils?”
✗
Power-pose → causal confidence / hormones
Disavowed by the lead author (Carney 2016); torso unsensable anyway.
✗
Clinical stress / anxiety / depression labels
App Review 5.1.x — affect must stay non-diagnostic.
✗
Self-touch → lying
DePaulo 2003: cues to deception average d≈.25 — no Pinocchio's nose.
✗
Gesture-emblem dictionary
“Crossed arms = defensive” — and arms/torso are unsensable regardless.

09 — TOOL-VERIFIED EVIDENCE, NEVER VIBES

Science & evidence

Every construct is mechanistically anchored. The confidence tag travels with the claim — if the research hedges, the app hedges. These are the load-bearing citations, color-coded by how much weight they can bear.

EstablishedProbableSpeculative / gated

Body breaks valence ties

Est.

At peak intensity, isolated faces are near-chance — the body discriminates, and its valence is misattributed onto the face. Justifies dynamic channel weighting.

Aviezer, Trope & Todorov 2012 · Science 338:1225

Suppression → flat face + arousal

Est.

Expressive suppression yields a flat face with rising sympathetic activation (SCL Δ 0.86 vs 0.33 µS, F(1,81)=6.19, p<.05) — the mechanism behind F1 Composure-under-load.

Gross & Levenson 1993 · JPSP 64:970

The head-pitch AU4 “imposter”

Est.

A head-down tilt manufactures a false V-brow that reads angrier/more dominant — so down-pitch must discount geometric anger. Ladder step 0.

Witkower & Tracy 2019 Psych Science 30:893 / 2020 Emotion

Pride / shame displays are innate

Est.

Head-back expansion and head-down submission appear in congenitally-blind athletes — cross-cultural, spontaneous. Read as head-orientation, hedged as felt-word glosses.

Tracy & Matsumoto 2008 · PNAS 105:11655

Voice = arousal, not valence

Est.

Arousal lives robustly in F0 mean/variance, intensity and tempo; valence is markedly inconsistent. Route prosody into arousal only.

Juslin & Laukka 2003 · Psych Bulletin 129:770

Visual-only accuracy ceiling

Est.

Design to this: valence CCC ≈ 0.516 (strong), arousal CCC ≈ 0.264 (weak). Face arousal must be shown with visibly wider uncertainty or rebuilt from motion/voice.

Allognon 2020 · IJCNN (visual-only V/A CCC)

The constructionist honesty bar

Est.

Face→emotion has weak reliability, specificity & generalizability — a bare categorical label is indefensible. Output must be posterior + V/A + confidence + baseline + context.

Barrett et al. 2019 · PSPI 20:1

Body = arousal >> valence

Est.

Across bodily-expression corpora, arousal is recoverable; valence only weakly, via approach/withdrawal + expansion/contraction. Sets the hands/head job description.

Kleinsmith & Bianchi-Berthouze 2013 · Wallbott 1998

Anger is approach-motivated

Est.

Motivational direction is dissociable from valence: anger is negative but approach; sadness/shame are withdrawal. The tie-breaker valence alone can't make.

Harmon-Jones & Allen 1998 · Carver & Harmon-Jones 2009

Gaze-aversion = load, not evasion

Est.

Looking away while thinking is cognitive load, never a deception tell — reframes any “looking away” cue toward the effort axis.

Doherty-Sneddon & Phelps 2005

Duchenne is an intensity artifact

Est.

AU6 doesn't separate felt from posed (70% felt vs 83% posed smiles were Duchenne). Drop genuine-vs-fake; use cross-channel corroboration.

Girard 2021 · Krumhuber & Manstead 2009

Why the refused list is refused

Est.

Cues to deception average d ≈ .25 — no Pinocchio's nose; NLP eye-accessing “tells” are debunked. The scientific basis for the never-ship list.

DePaulo 2003 · Wiseman 2012 · PLOS ONE

Per-user calibration is mandatory

Prob.

Hand-only affect classified 50–87% within-user but collapsed to chance leave-one-user-out (N=22) — empirical proof baselines can't be generic.

Chua 2024 · “Motion as Emotion,” arXiv 2409.12921

Semantic-space vocabulary

Est.

Emotion is high-dimensional gradients & blends, not crisp categories — the narrator speaks “between focused and tense,” never a single-label verdict.

Cowen & Keltner 2017 · PNAS 114:E7900

Ordered display sequences

Gated

Embarrassment/pride unfold as ordered ~1–3 s sequences — but need ≥30–60 Hz; the ~15 Hz pipeline can't sample them. Scoped to a stretch tier, not launch.

Keltner 1995 · gated on confirmed Hz
